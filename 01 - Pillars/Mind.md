## ⚙️ Mechanism

- **Trigger:** Duolingo at breakfast — first thing. ✅ This works and is the model for every other pillar.
- **Counter:** the Duolingo streak (external) + `duolingo_min`, `duolingo_lessons`, `duolingo_level`, `pages_read`.
- **Known failure mode:** ⚠️ **the streak lies.** 555 days unbroken, but many days were 15 min or a single lesson, and level 60 is far off. A binary counter rewards showing up, not progress. Hence tracking **minutes and level**, not the streak.

Reading has the opposite problem: no trigger at all, and 2025's version of it optimised the wrong thing —

> *"no ano passado eu para provar a mim mesmo, eu li por ler... não interagi com os livros como poderia ter interagido. Eu quero aprender e aplicar o que estes livros ensinam."*

That is why every book gets a note in [[06 - Library/README|Library]] with an **Applied** section. A book with an empty Applied table did not count, regardless of pages.
